ABOUT THIS CAREER EXPLORER

Beyond the Usual Career List

This evidence-informed resource was developed to broaden the career options shown in general Biological Sciences guidance. It responds to career-awareness gaps identified in a postgraduate student survey and draws on three complementary sources:

01 · Graduate career journeys

Publicly described education and work histories identified through targeted LinkedIn career mapping. These show examples of where graduates have worked and how their careers developed.

02 · Career guidance

The Wits Biological Sciences careers resources and City of Cape Town environmental careers guidance were consulted during career selection and mapping for possible roles and sectors.

03 · Municipal scarce skills

The City of Cape Town 2025 Scarce Skills List was used to identify selected skill areas appearing in the mapped careers. This is municipality-specific evidence, not a national vacancy or employment forecast.

Career journeys are anonymised examples drawn from publicly described profiles; listed roles and qualifications are not necessarily mandatory or sequential. Career-stage bands are illustrative, not verified requirements. Scarce-skill signals are municipal and should not be interpreted as national employment forecasts.
